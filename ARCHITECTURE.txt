   +--> add_bill -> data/bills.json {name, amount, due_date, recurrence, category, reminder_at}
   +--> check_due_bills -> reads bills.json, filters due within N days
   +--> scan_receipt -> regex parses amount/items, calls add_bill/manage_groceries internally
   +--> manage_groceries -> data/grocery.json {items[], added_at}
   +--> audit_subscriptions -> groups bills by category=entertainment/subscription, sums monthly, detects duplicates
   +--> search_best_practices -> Tavily/Exa (in production), for hackathon demonstrates specific query generation
